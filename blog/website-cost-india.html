Understanding Website Development Pricing Models in India

One of the most frequent questions business owners ask when planning their online presence is: "How much will a professional website actually cost?" In India, website development prices range anywhere from ₹10,000 for basic template sites to over ₹2,00,000 for custom web applications and e-commerce platforms.

The final price depends on key factors: total page count, visual customization, custom functional requirements, database integrations, content creation, and search engine optimization (SEO).

"A website is not an expense—it is your company's digital sales representative available 24/7. Investing in high-speed clean engineering pays for itself through increased lead inquiries and conversions."

Average Website Cost Breakdown in India (2026 Rates)

Website Type Page Range Estimated Price (INR) Timeline
Starter Business Website 5 Pages ₹12,000 – ₹20,000 1–2 Weeks
Growth Business Website 8–12 Pages ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 2–3 Weeks
E-Commerce Store Platform 15+ Products ₹45,000 – ₹90,000 3–4 Weeks
Custom Enterprise SaaS / App Custom Portals ₹75,000 – ₹2,50,000+ 4–8 Weeks

Key Cost Components To Consider

  • Domain Registration: ₹800 – ₹1,500 / year (.com or .in).
  • Cloud Hosting & CDN: ₹2,500 – ₹10,000 / year (Fast SSD servers, SSL certificate).
  • UI/UX Design & Development: One-time fee covering custom graphics, HTML5 layout, and responsive CSS.
  • Maintenance & Security: Regular backups, software patches, and content updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there hidden annual renewal fees?

No. Beyond standard domain renewal (~₹1,000/yr) and cloud web hosting, there are no mandatory hidden charges.
