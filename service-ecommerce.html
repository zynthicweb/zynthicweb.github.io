Frequently Asked Questions

Which platform should I choose: Shopify, WooCommerce, or Custom?

Shopify is ideal for fast launch and low maintenance, WooCommerce offers maximum customization for WordPress users, and a custom Next.js/Laravel backend is best for enterprise platforms requiring unique business logic and high throughput. We guide you to the right fit based on your catalog size and budget.

How do you secure payment transactions on custom e-commerce sites?

We implement PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways, enforce SSL/TLS tokenized checkouts, and never store raw credit card details on your servers. Webhook handlers are secured with secret signature verification to prevent fraudulent orders.
